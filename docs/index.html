I

The problem

Your first tour of Yharnam
should not be a compilation pass.

Bloodborne's PC ports compile graphics work as new content appears. World Warper turns that unavoidable first encounter into a controlled, resumable run—without touching the player's live save or substituting another fork's incompatible cache.
